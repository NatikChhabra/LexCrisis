LexCrisis

Read the scenarios

Every scenario is synthetic, written by someone who has never seen a real matter file. They are published in full — six intake clients with their conflict rules, ten privilege documents with their ground truth, six crisis events with deadlines — so the flaws are findable without reading Python.
BSA s.132Professional communications — attorney–client privilege
BSA s.134Confidential communication with legal advisers
BCI 22, 33Bar Council conflict rules driving the intake decisions
The classification vocabulary includes work_product, a US doctrine with no clean Indian equivalent, and the procedure throughout is US-style discovery. This may be US process wearing Indian citations.

How scoring works

Four deterministic verifier columns. No LLM judge anywhere in the loop.

process_compliance
deadline_or_latency
